Who it’s for

Students

2nd to 4th year CS and IT students preparing for placements. Start at lesson 1 of either track; nothing is assumed beyond basic Python.

Working engineers

0–5 years of experience, preparing for SDE-1/SDE-2 interviews at product companies, including the machine-coding (LLD) round.

Why this course

  • One path, both tracks. DSA, System Design, LLD and interview craft in one ordered course, with one hub for practice.
  • Animated, in Hinglish. Code-drawn animations, narrated in Hinglish, with English text and Python code on screen.
  • Indian case studies. UPI, Tatkal booking, IPL streaming, 10-minute delivery, fantasy-cricket leaderboards.
  • Intuition over memorising. Each DSA lesson teaches the pattern with a story, then gives problems from first-try to stretch.

How each lesson works

  1. Watch the long lesson (8–20 min) or its 60–90 s Short.
  2. When a lesson is out, comment its keyword on the Reel or Short to get the one-page cheat-sheet in your DMs.
  3. Solve the practice problems on the lesson page and tick them off.
  4. Go deeper with the linked sources when you want the full theory.

This is a learning design for a system like the one named. It is not a description of any company’s internal architecture.

What you’ll learn

  • Seat inventory modelling; holds with expiry
  • Contention: locks, optimistic concurrency, queues/virtual waiting room
  • Fairness and bot defence at a burst

The story behind it

10:00:00 AM Tatkal window: lakhs of people, a few hundred berths. Two people tap the same berth in the same millisecond — who gets it, and how does the system stay up?

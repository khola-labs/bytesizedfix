What you’ll learn

  • Client-server model; IP addresses and ports

The story behind it

Mumbai's dabbawalas: your tiffin (data) is split, coded, passed between trains and handlers (routers) and re-assembled at your desk. TCP is the dabbawala who confirms every box; UDP is the newspaper boy who throws and moves on.
